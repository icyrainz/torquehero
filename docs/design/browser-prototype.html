Controls and layers

Plug the wheel in, press any button on it so the browser exposes it, then click Learn and move that control. Uncheck a layer to let the song play it for you. Bindings are remembered in this browser.

What each layer asks

MelodyThe road bends with the lead line. Stay on it, and be inside each gate as it crosses the line. Off the road, the melody goes muffled.
KickLeft shoulder pads. Stomp the brake on them. How hard you press is how loud the kick is.
HatsSmall left shoulder ticks on the offbeats. Tap the clutch.
SwellThe throttle is an expression pedal. Follow the target on the gauge. It sets the pad volume.
StabsRight shoulder hexagons with a number. Slam the shifter into that gate on the beat.
FillsRight shoulder chevrons. Left and right paddle in the order shown.
RiserPull the handbrake at the start of the purple band, hold it, release exactly on the drop.
SpinA ring on the road. One full turn of the wheel, either way, within the bar.
EchoThe base plays a phrase by turning the wheel on its own. Then the road goes dark and you play it back.

Force feedback plan

MomentWhat the base does
Every beat80 ms sine burst. Downbeats hit harder.
Section weightSteering resistance follows the dynamics. Chorus is heavy, breakdown is light.
RiserTorque winds up against you while the handbrake is held. The drop releases it.
EchoThe base turns the wheel through the phrase. Hands off, feel it, then repeat.
Perfect gate60 ms kick in the direction you steered.
Miss250 ms low rumble.

The browser cannot drive force feedback, so the meter at the top right shows the torque the base would receive. The desktop build sends these effects to the base through SDL2 haptics.
